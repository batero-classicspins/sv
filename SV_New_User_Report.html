Main drop-off points, by severity

1 Biggest drop: players enter a slot machine and leave before level 2

  • 22% of players who entered a machine (668 users) never reached level 2; most opened a single machine and quit.
  • About 35% of all new users stayed at level 1, with further drop-off at levels 2–4.
  • Review the first minutes in the machine: starting coins, early win rate, tutorial, time to first level-up.

A. Never finished loading (79 users)

Median 15 seconds, mostly stuck at the lobby while loading. Almost no crashes, and no specific device or Android version stands out. This looks like slow loading rather than crashing: measure tunning_data load time on mobile networks.

B. Loaded but never entered a machine (55 users, 84% uninstalled within an hour)

Last event is usually the 5,000-coin install gift, a store open (6), or a card-collection load error (3). Players are likely blocked by a popup/store/gift in the lobby. Test sending new users straight into a machine and disabling first-session popups.

C. Entered a machine, never reached level 2 (117 users): see Report 3

Almost every new user is placed in Tropical Island; 31% of them never reach level 2.

Purchases: 30 buy taps, 0 purchases

  • The most tapped item is the $5.99 Monthly Pass (promo_lobby), often 2–3 times before cancelling: real intent. Possible causes: no payment method on the Play account, or a price mismatch between game and Google Play sheet.
  • Level 1–3 players tap $99–$199 packs (likely by accident); the $1.99 starter pack almost never appears for this cohort.
  • One PRICE_NOT_FOUND on the Monthly Pass: check the product configuration in Google Play.

1-spin group: they leave exactly at the forced bet increase

All made a first spin at 100 and won a tiny amount. The tutorial then asks for a 2,000 bet; they quit within ~7 seconds and uninstall ~16 seconds later. Only 1 opened the store. 11 came back later, loaded into the lobby and left without spinning.

0-spin group: they leave almost instantly

The machine appears; ~6 seconds later they quit. No crashes. Install-to-uninstall takes about 1 minute, a sign of low-intent installs (accidental ad clicks, common in Google App Campaigns on display/in-game placements), or a tutorial overlay that confuses players. Verify the first screen directly.

2+ spin group: the tutorial drops them after the scripted win

Spin 2 at the forced bet always wins big (21,000–34,000 coins), yet they still do not reach level 2. They exit to the lobby (core_abandoned); some open the store and leave. The tutorial does not carry them through to level 2 and its 10,000-coin reward.

Other events

  • app_remove: 90 of 117 (77%), mostly 1–4 minutes after install.
  • No crashes, no significant meta_failed, almost no store/buy activity.
Recommendations
  1. A/B test the forced bet step (38 users quit there in ~7 sec): remove it in the first session, or give the big win on spin 1 at bet 100 and only then suggest raising the bet.
  2. After the scripted win, take players straight to level 2 and award the 10,000 coins instead of dropping them in the lobby.
  3. 0-spin users look like low-quality traffic: review placement reporting for SV-GP, or optimize the campaign for an in-game event (level_up, 5th spin) instead of installs.

4Do players go bankrupt before level 5?

Fresh accounts only (starting with 5,000 coins), spins before reaching level 5.

No. Bankruptcy is not why new players leave before level 5. It is rare, the game refills coins within seconds, and players who go broke actually progress further than those who don’t.
Correction to earlier analysis An earlier version stated that a 5,000-coin player could only spin 1–2 times at a 2,000–5,000 bet before running out. That was wrong: win events carry a slightly different timestamp than bet events, and reinstalled accounts with old balances had been mixed in. With both fixed, the tutorial is safe.

Players who go broke are the more engaged, bigger-betting players. The only harmful case, “broke, no free coins, never spun again”, is 11 users (0.5%), only 1 US paid user; most (9) are iOS users at level 1.

Conclusions
  1. The early coin economy and refills work well; bankruptcy is not a meaningful churn cause before level 5.
  2. The real drop-off remains the tutorial “raise your bet” step: show the big win on spin 1, or make the payoff of raising the bet obvious.
  3. Out-of-coins is a monetization moment: broke players open the store (47%) and tap buy (16%) far more than average (~7%). Show the $1.99 starter pack there.
  4. Possible iOS bug: 8 iOS users went hard broke at level 1 betting 100, and 9 were stuck with no refill. Check the out-of-coins offer / free bonus on iOS at level 1.

Method & data notes

  • Cohort: users with a first_open event between Sep 20 and Oct 4, 2026. Oct 5–6 had not yet been exported to BigQuery and no intraday tables exist.
  • Excluded: .DEV builds, users with no country, Vietnam (likely internal devices).
  • US paid: country = United States and traffic_source.medium = cpc (all Android, campaign SV-GP).
  • Funnel steps: meta_started/meta_completed (first_loading, tunning_data), core_started, level_up, store_entered, IAP_start, IAP_completed (non-test, receipt = Success).
  • Retention: session_start on exactly day N after install; D1 uses installs through Oct 3, D3 through Oct 1, D7 through Sep 27.
  • Bankruptcy: each spend_virtual_currency paired with the following spin_main win; fresh accounts only (≤ 10,000 coins before first spin).
  • iOS does not send app_remove; uninstall rates are not comparable across platforms.
